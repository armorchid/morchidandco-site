Settlement

Indefinite leave to remain and the new earned settlement rules

The route to settlement in the UK is changing. We explain where you stand today, what the earned settlement reforms may mean for you, and prepare your ILR application properly.

What is changing: earned settlement

On 2 November 2026 the government confirmed its earned settlement model. The standard qualifying period for indefinite leave to remain (ILR) will rise from five to 10 years for most people, including many already in the UK. Changes to the Immigration Rules are expected from December 2026, with implementation during 2027. Start dates and the treatment of pending applications are not yet confirmed.

Proposed pathways

  • 3 years: earnings of at least £125,141 a year in three of the previous four years
  • 5 years: earnings of at least £50,271 a year in three of the previous five years
  • 10 years: earnings of at least £12,571 a year in five of the previous seven years
  • 20 years: adults with specified immigration breaches (such as overstaying) or recent use of specified public funds

Couples may combine earnings to meet a £25,142 household minimum. B2 English and the Life in the UK test are expected for most applicants, and an adult UK criminal record will lead to refusal.

Who is expected to keep a five-year route

Eligible partners and children of British citizens, EU Settlement Scheme pre-settled status holders, UK Ancestry and Hong Kong BN(O) visa holders, some doctors, nurses and teachers, and some care workers who arrived before the May 2025 White Paper.

The long residence route is closing

The 10-year long residence route will close, and time on Student, Graduate and some other temporary routes will not count towards settlement. If you are close to 10 years' lawful residence, get advice now.

ILR under the current rules

Most work and family routes currently lead to ILR after five years' continuous residence. You usually need to meet the continuous residence and absence rules, pass the Life in the UK test, meet the English requirement and the route's own conditions. The Home Office fee is £3,226 from 8 April 2026. Our fixed fee for an ILR application is £700. Long residence and private life applications are £800.

After ILR: British citizenship

Most people can apply to naturalise 12 months after ILR, or straight away if married to a British citizen. See our British citizenship service.

Frequently asked questions

Is ILR now 10 years?

The government has confirmed 10 years as the standard route, but it is not yet in the Immigration Rules. Until the new rules take effect, the current qualifying periods apply.

Will earned settlement apply to people already in the UK?

The government has said there will be no general exemption for existing visa holders, with transitional arrangements for some groups. Details will be in the new rules.

Should I apply for ILR before the rules change?

If you already qualify under the current rules, applying before the change may protect you, but the treatment of pending applications is not yet confirmed. A Settlement Check will tell you your options.

Does time on a Graduate visa count towards ILR?

Under the government's plans, time on the Student and Graduate routes will not count towards settlement.
