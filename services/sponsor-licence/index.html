Sponsor licence applications for UK employers

We help small and medium-sized businesses get a Home Office sponsor licence, recruit Skilled Workers correctly and stay compliant, so your licence and your workers' visas are safe.

Do you need a sponsor licence?

A UK employer needs a sponsor licence to employ most overseas workers on the Skilled Worker, Global Business Mobility or Temporary Work routes. Without one, you cannot assign the Certificates of Sponsorship that workers need to apply for their visas. We regularly help restaurants, care providers, retailers, IT businesses, construction firms and family businesses.

Sponsor licence requirements

  • A genuine organisation, operating lawfully in the UK, with the required documents (for example business bank statement, employer's liability insurance, VAT and HMRC registration, premises evidence)
  • Suitable key personnel: an Authorising Officer, a Key Contact and at least one Level 1 User
  • HR systems able to meet sponsor duties: right to work checks, record-keeping, monitoring attendance and reporting changes
  • Genuine vacancies that meet the skill and salary rules
  • No unspent relevant convictions or history of non-compliance among key personnel

Skilled Worker rules employers must know in 2026

  • Skill level: most new roles must be at degree level (RQF 6) since 22 July 2025.
  • Salary: the general threshold has been £41,700 a year since July 2025, or the occupation's going rate if higher, with some lower rates for new entrants. The government has announced new occupation thresholds from spring 2027.
  • English: new Skilled Worker applicants need English at B2 for applications from 8 January 2026.
  • Care workers: overseas recruitment of care workers closed on 22 July 2025, with transitional rules for workers already in the UK.
  • Costs: the employer must pay the Immigration Skills Charge and the Certificate of Sponsorship fee and cannot pass them to the worker.

How the sponsor licence process works

  1. Eligibility check: we review your business, roles and salaries.
  2. Document pack: we tell you exactly which documents to gather and check them.
  3. Online application: we complete the application and the supporting submission.
  4. Compliance readiness: we prepare you for a possible Home Office pre-licence visit.
  5. After approval: we help you assign Certificates of Sponsorship and set up your sponsor management system.

Standard processing usually takes around eight weeks; a priority service is available for an extra fee.

Sponsor compliance: protect your licence

The Home Office tightened its sponsor guidance on 5 March 2026 and has made clear that a licence can be revoked even where breaches are unintentional. New reporting obligations took effect in April 2026. We help with compliance reviews, right to work checks, reporting changes on time and preparing for compliance visits.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a sponsor licence take?

Standard processing usually takes around eight weeks from submission of the application and documents. A priority service is available for an extra fee.

Can a small business get a sponsor licence?

Yes. Many sponsors are small businesses. You must show you are genuine, trading lawfully and able to meet sponsor duties.

What happens if my sponsor licence is revoked?

Sponsored workers' permission is usually cut short and they must find a new sponsor, switch visa or leave. Revocation also blocks a new application for a period.

Can I pass the Immigration Skills Charge to my worker?

No. The Immigration Skills Charge and the Certificate of Sponsorship fee must be paid by the employer.
