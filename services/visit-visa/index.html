Visit visa or ETA?

Visa nationals, including nationals of Morocco, Algeria, Egypt and many other countries, need a visit visa before travelling. Non-visa nationals, including EU, US and Canadian citizens, now need an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA). Since 25 February 2026, airlines must refuse boarding to anyone who needs an ETA and does not have one.

Visit visa requirements

  • A genuine purpose: family visit, tourism, business meetings, private medical treatment or a short course
  • Intention to leave the UK at the end of the visit, usually within six months
  • Enough funds for the trip, clearly explained, from you or a sponsor in the UK
  • Strong ties to your home country: work, studies, family, property

Long-term visit visas valid for 2, 5 or 10 years are available, but each stay is normally limited to six months.

Why visit visas are refused

  • Large deposits that are not explained, or bank statements that do not match the stated income
  • Weak evidence of employment or ties at home
  • An unclear purpose or itinerary, or inconsistent details between applicant and sponsor
  • Previous refusals or overstays not disclosed

Visit visa refusals normally have no right of appeal, so the best response is a stronger fresh application that answers every reason given. See visa refusals.

Documents

  • Passport and travel history
  • Bank statements, payslips, employer letter or business documents
  • Sponsor's invitation letter, proof of status and finances if a family member is supporting you
  • Evidence of the purpose: hospital letter, conference invitation, event details

Frequently asked questions

Do Moroccans need a visa to visit the UK?

Yes. Moroccan nationals are visa nationals and need a visit visa before travelling to the UK.

How long can I stay on a visit visa?

Normally up to six months per visit, even on a long-term visit visa.

Can I appeal a visit visa refusal?

Usually not. The normal route is a fresh application that addresses each reason for refusal.

Can I marry in the UK on a visit visa?

You can give notice of marriage only on a marriage visitor visa or fiancé(e) visa. Marrying on a standard visit visa is not permitted.
