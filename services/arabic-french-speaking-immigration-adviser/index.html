Who we help

We advise Arabic-speaking and French-speaking clients across the UK and abroad, including families in Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Egypt, the Gulf states, France, Belgium, Switzerland and West Africa, and their relatives in the UK.

  • British and settled partners bringing a spouse or fiancé(e) from North Africa or the Middle East: see spouse visas
  • Parents and relatives visiting family in the UK: see visit visas
  • EU citizens from France and Belgium with EU Settlement Scheme questions
  • Employers recruiting French or Arabic-speaking staff: see sponsor licences
  • People facing refusals, asylum or human rights issues: see refusals and appeals

Why the language matters

  • You can explain your family history, relationship and finances fully and in detail
  • Documents in Arabic and French can be reviewed before certified translation, saving time and cost
  • Family members abroad can join the consultation and understand what is needed
  • Your application is still prepared in English, as the Home Office requires

Frequently asked questions

Do you give immigration advice in Arabic?

Yes. Our adviser speaks Arabic, French and English.

Can my family abroad join the consultation?

Yes. Consultations are by video or phone, so family members in another country can join.

Are applications submitted in Arabic or French?

No. Home Office applications are made in English, and documents in other languages need certified translations. We tell you which documents to translate.
