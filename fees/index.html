How our fees work

  • Our fees are for our professional work. They do not include Home Office application fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge, the Immigration Skills Charge, translation costs or other third-party costs.
  • We confirm the exact fee, what it covers and when it is payable in a client care letter before we start.
  • Our hourly rate of £150 (£15 per 6-minute unit) applies only if you withdraw instructions part way through a case, or if the work changes from what was agreed. We always tell you in advance how many hours we expect it to take.
  • If you instruct us online or by phone, you have a 14-day cancellation period. If you ask us to start work within that period, you may need to pay for work already done.
  • If legal aid or free advice may be available to you, for example in some asylum cases, we will tell you.
