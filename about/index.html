About the adviser

Morchid & Co is run by Abderrahmane Morchid, an immigration adviser authorised by the Immigration Advice Authority at Level 3, the highest level.

Abderrahmane Morchid

Abderrahmane Morchid founded Morchid & Co in 2017. He is registered with the Immigration Advice Authority (IAA), formerly the OISC, and authorised at Level 3, which covers advice, applications, appeals and representation at the First-tier Tribunal and Upper Tribunal.

He speaks English, Arabic and French, and works with clients across the UK and worldwide entirely online, by video, phone, WhatsApp and email. Clients deal with him personally from the first consultation to the final decision.

Areas of work

  • Spouse, partner and family visas
  • Refusals, administrative reviews and appeals
  • Indefinite leave to remain and earned settlement
  • British citizenship
  • Sponsor licences and Skilled Worker visas
  • Visit visas and ETA
  • Asylum and human rights claims
