Graduate visa to be cut to 18 months from January 2027

For applications made from 1 January 2027, the Graduate visa will last 18 months for most graduates instead of two years. PhD graduates keep three years.

Event date: 1 January 2027Published: 21 November 2026By: Mr A. Morchid, IAA Level 3 adviser

Statement of Changes HC 1333 confirmed that the Graduate route will be shortened from two years to 18 months for applications made from 1 January 2027. PhD graduates keep three years.

In the 2026 Autumn Budget the government also announced plans for a two-year Graduate visa for graduates of high-compliance universities with strong labour-market outcomes. The list of qualifying institutions has not yet been published. Under the earned settlement plans, time on the Graduate route will not count towards settlement.

What this means for you: Students finishing courses in 2026 who are eligible to apply before 1 January 2027 may benefit from the longer two-year visa. Plan your application dates carefully.
