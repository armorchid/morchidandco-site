Immigration Rules changes from 8 October 2026: the key points

A wide package of changes laid on 3 September 2026 took effect on 8 October, affecting the EU Settlement Scheme, Skilled Workers, long residence, visitors and suitability.

Event date: 8 October 2026Published: 21 November 2026By: Mr A. Morchid, IAA Level 3 adviser

The Home Office published Statement of Changes HC 584 on 3 September 2026, with most changes taking effect on 8 October 2026.

Main changes

  • Suitability: a new refusal ground covers applicants who have acted to frustrate immigration control.
  • Long residence: if a long residence application fails, the caseworker may consider it under Appendix FM or Appendix Private Life instead.
  • EU Settlement Scheme: administrative review was removed for EUSS decisions, and pre-settled status may in some cases be extended or upgraded without a new application.
  • Skilled Worker: relaxed work conditions for confirmed victims of modern slavery.
  • Hong Kong BN(O): clarified settlement requirements.
  • Visitors: new permitted activities for Erasmus+ participants.

Religious worker and minister of religion changes followed on 29 October 2026.

What this means for you: If you applied under one of these routes around October 2026, check whether the old or new rules apply to your decision. Some changes include transitional protection.
