Refugee status now reviewed every 30 months for new asylum claims

For adults claiming asylum from 2 March 2026, refugee status is granted for 30 months instead of five years, under the new core protection model.

Event date: 2 March 2026Published: 21 November 2026By: Mr A. Morchid, IAA Level 3 adviser

From 2 March 2026, refugee status became temporary and subject to review every 30 months for adults and accompanied children claiming asylum from that date. It replaces the previous five-year grant.

At each review, protection is renewed for refugees who still face danger. Those whose countries are considered safe will be expected to return. Under the reforms announced in November 2025, refugees on core protection face a 20-year wait for settlement unless they switch to a work or study route.

What this means for you: If you claimed asylum on or after 2 March 2026, your grant and route to settlement differ from earlier claims. Get advice on whether you could later move to another route.
