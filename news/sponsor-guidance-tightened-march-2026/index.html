Sponsor guidance tightened: licences at risk even for unintentional breaches

Updated Home Office sponsor guidance, effective immediately from 5 March 2026, makes clear that a licence can be revoked even where breaches are unintentional.

Event date: 5 March 2026Published: 21 November 2026By: Mr A. Morchid, IAA Level 3 adviser

On 5 March 2026 the Home Secretary published a wide-ranging Statement of Changes (HC 1691). Alongside it, the Home Office published updated sponsor guidance, effective immediately, tightening compliance expectations across Skilled Worker and Temporary Worker routes.

The updated guidance makes clear that sponsor licences may be revoked even where breaches are unintentional, and puts renewed emphasis on governance and record-keeping.

What this means for you: Employers holding a sponsor licence should carry out a compliance review now. Mistakes in right to work checks, reporting or salary can put the licence, and your workers' visas, at risk.
