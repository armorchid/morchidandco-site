Wider protection for victims of domestic abuse on partner visas

From 8 October 2026 the domestic abuse provisions in the family rules cover more people, including some dependent children over 18 and people abandoned overseas.

Event date: 8 October 2026By: Mr A. Morchid, IAA Level 3 adviser

The family rules have long allowed some partners whose relationship broke down because of domestic abuse to apply to settle. From 8 October 2026 these provisions were widened.

  • They cover relationship breakdown caused by abuse.
  • They extend to pre-settled EU Settlement Scheme family members.
  • They include dependent children, in some cases over 18.
  • They cover people who have been abandoned overseas.

The main applicant must generally be 18 or over.

What this means for you: If you are on a partner visa and your relationship has ended because of abuse, you may have a route to stay. Speak to an adviser in confidence. If you are in danger, call 999.
