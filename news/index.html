Immigration news

UK immigration news and updates

The main UK immigration changes of the past year, explained in plain English by an IAA-regulated adviser, and what they mean for you.

How to read these dates: each article shows the event date, when the change was announced or takes effect, and the date we published it. We update articles when the rules change.
Study

Graduate visa to be cut to 18 months from January 2027

For applications made from 1 January 2027, the Graduate visa will last 18 months for most graduates instead of two years. PhD graduates keep three years.

Event date: 1 January 2027EU Settlement Scheme

EU Settlement Scheme: new document requirement from 9 December 2026

Some non-EEA family members applying to the EU Settlement Scheme from inside the UK will need a valid passport or another specified document from 9 December 2026.

Event date: 9 December 2026Study

Student visa maintenance funds rise from 30 November 2026

Students applying from 30 November 2026 must show slightly more money for living costs: £1,570 a month in London and £1,203 a month elsewhere.

Event date: 30 November 2026Settlement

Earned settlement confirmed: 10 years becomes the standard route to ILR

The government has confirmed its earned settlement model. Most people will wait 10 years for indefinite leave to remain, with shorter routes for higher earners and some key groups.

Event date: 2 November 2026Work and business

Autumn Budget 2026: what changes for Skilled Workers and employers

The Budget announced new Skilled Worker salary rules from spring 2027, more time for some workers to find a new sponsor, and wider High Potential Individual eligibility.

Event date: 28 October 2026Rule changes

Immigration Rules changes from 8 October 2026: the key points

A wide package of changes laid on 3 September 2026 took effect on 8 October, affecting the EU Settlement Scheme, Skilled Workers, long residence, visitors and suitability.

Event date: 8 October 2026EU Settlement Scheme

EU Settlement Scheme: administrative review removed

From 8 October 2026 refused EU Settlement Scheme applicants can no longer ask for an administrative review. Appeals remain the main challenge route.

Event date: 8 October 2026Family

Wider protection for victims of domestic abuse on partner visas

From 8 October 2026 the domestic abuse provisions in the family rules cover more people, including some dependent children over 18 and people abandoned overseas.

Event date: 8 October 2026Rule changes

Summer 2026 rule changes: Graduate families and criminality refusals

Changes laid on 9 July 2026 let UK-born children of Graduate visa holders apply as dependants and extended criminality refusals to suspended sentences of 12 months or more.

Event date: 3 August 2026eVisas

Visa stickers withdrawn: new arrivals now travel on eVisas

UK Visas and Immigration stopped placing vignette stickers in passports for successful new applicants on 1 July 2026. Status is now proved digitally.

Event date: 1 July 2026EU Settlement Scheme

Pre-settled status holders upgraded to settled status automatically

An enhanced Home Office system went live on 9 April 2026, converting eligible pre-settled status holders to settled status without an application.

Event date: 9 April 2026Fees

Home Office application fees increased on 8 April 2026

Most UK immigration and nationality fees went up on 8 April 2026. ILR now costs £3,226 and naturalisation £1,709.

Event date: 8 April 2026Work and business

Spring 2026: new sponsor reporting duties and right to work changes

Most of Statement of Changes HC 1695, laid on 13 March 2026, took effect on 8 April 2026, adding new reporting obligations for sponsors.

Event date: 8 April 2026Work and business

Sponsor guidance tightened: licences at risk even for unintentional breaches

Updated Home Office sponsor guidance, effective immediately from 5 March 2026, makes clear that a licence can be revoked even where breaches are unintentional.

Event date: 5 March 2026Asylum

Refugee status now reviewed every 30 months for new asylum claims

For adults claiming asylum from 2 March 2026, refugee status is granted for 30 months instead of five years, under the new core protection model.

Event date: 2 March 2026Visits

No permission, no travel: ETA now enforced for visa-free visitors

Since 25 February 2026, visitors from 85 nationalities who do not need a visa cannot travel to the UK without an Electronic Travel Authorisation.

Event date: 25 February 2026Work and business

Skilled Worker English requirement rises to B2

New Skilled Worker, Scale-up and High Potential Individual applicants must now show English at B2 level, up from B1, for applications from 8 January 2026.

Event date: 8 January 2026Work and business

Immigration Skills Charge increase for sponsoring employers

A 32% increase to the Immigration Skills Charge paid by sponsoring employers was due to take effect on 16 December 2025.

Event date: 16 December 2025Settlement

Earned settlement consultation opened

The Home Office opened its consultation on extending the qualifying period for settlement on 20 November 2025, proposing that changes apply to people already in the UK.

Event date: 20 November 2025Asylum

Restoring Order and Control: the government's new asylum model

On 17 November 2025 the government published its asylum and returns policy, moving to temporary refugee status and a 20-year route to settlement on core protection.

Event date: 17 November 2025
