Immigration Skills Charge increase for sponsoring employers

A 32% increase to the Immigration Skills Charge paid by sponsoring employers was due to take effect on 16 December 2025.

Event date: 16 December 2025Published: 21 November 2026By: Mr A. Morchid, IAA Level 3 adviser

The Immigration Skills Charge (ISC) is paid by employers who sponsor skilled workers from overseas. The government announced in its May 2025 White Paper that the charge would rise, and a 32% increase was expected to take effect on 16 December 2025.

The charge is paid per worker, for each year of the Certificate of Sponsorship, and must be paid by the employer. It cannot be passed on to the worker. From 28 October 2026, an employer paying the ISC does not create a taxable benefit in kind for the worker.

What this means for you: Employers should include the higher ISC in recruitment budgets for every sponsored role, alongside the Certificate of Sponsorship fee.
