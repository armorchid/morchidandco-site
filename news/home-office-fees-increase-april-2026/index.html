Home Office application fees increased on 8 April 2026

Most UK immigration and nationality fees went up on 8 April 2026. ILR now costs £3,226 and naturalisation £1,709.

Event date: 8 April 2026Published: 21 November 2026By: Mr A. Morchid, IAA Level 3 adviser

The Home Office increased most immigration and nationality fees on 8 April 2026. Key examples:

  • Spouse or partner visa from outside the UK: £2,064 (was £1,938).
  • Spouse or partner extension inside the UK: £1,407 (was £1,321).
  • Indefinite leave to remain: £3,226 (was £3,029).
  • Naturalisation as a British citizen: £1,709 (was £1,605).
  • Child registration as a British citizen: £1,000 (was £1,214).
  • Skilled Worker visa up to 3 years, from outside the UK: £819 (was £769).

These figures do not include the Immigration Health Surcharge, biometric fees or passport fees.

What this means for you: Budget for both the Home Office fee and the Immigration Health Surcharge. Our professional fees are separate and listed on our fees page.
