No permission, no travel: ETA now enforced for visa-free visitors

Since 25 February 2026, visitors from 85 nationalities who do not need a visa cannot travel to the UK without an Electronic Travel Authorisation.

Event date: 25 February 2026Published: 21 November 2026By: Mr A. Morchid, IAA Level 3 adviser

From 25 February 2026 the UK began fully enforcing digital permission to travel. Visitors from 85 nationalities who do not need a visa, including the United States, Canada and France, cannot travel to the UK without an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA).

Airlines and other carriers must check through automated systems that passengers hold permission before boarding. Permission must be an ETA, an eVisa or other acceptable proof. British and Irish citizens are exempt. Dual British nationals should travel on a British passport or a passport with a certificate of entitlement.

What this means for you: If your family visits from Europe, North America or other visa-free countries, they must get an ETA before booking travel. Visa nationals, including Moroccan nationals, still need a visit visa.
